What is Rizqly?

Community Savings Made Simple

A ROSCA — also known as Kameti, Chit Fund, or Committee — is a group savings arrangement where every member contributes a fixed amount each cycle, and one member receives the full pot. Rizqly makes this completely paperless and transparent.

🏘️

Who is it for?

Community organisers, families, friends, and colleagues who run rotating savings groups and want a reliable digital record instead of paper ledgers or manual spreadsheets.

🎯

What problem does it solve?

Manual ROSCA tracking leads to disputes, forgotten payments, and missing records. Rizqly gives every committee a clear, timestamped ledger that all parties can trust.

📲

What does it run on?

Rizqly runs entirely on Android. It connects to your secure account in the cloud so your data is safe even if you switch phones. No desktop required.

Installation Guide

How to Install Rizqly on Android

Installing an APK is simple. Follow these steps to get Rizqly running on your device.

1

Download the APK

Tap the "Download Rizqly APK" button in the Download section above. Your browser will save the file to your Downloads folder.

2

Open the downloaded file

Go to your Downloads app or file manager, find Rizqly-1.0.0.apk, and tap it to open it.

3

Allow installation from this source

Android may display a prompt saying the app is from an unknown source. Tap Settings, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager, then return and try again.

4

Tap Install

Android will show you the app permissions. Tap Install to confirm. The installation takes a few seconds.

5

Open Rizqly

Tap Open on the installation screen, or find the Rizqly icon in your app drawer. Sign in to your account and you're ready to go.

⚠️ About the "Unknown Source" warning:
Android shows a warning when you install an app from outside Google Play. This is a standard Android security feature — it doesn't mean the app is unsafe. Rizqly is a direct-distribution app. You can safely allow the installation.
💡 Android version requirement:
Rizqly is built with React Native 0.81 and targets Android 8.0 (API 26) and above. Most Android phones from 2018 or later are compatible.
🔄 Updating Rizqly:
When a new version is released, download the latest APK from this page and install it over the existing app. Your data will be preserved.

Getting Started

Step 1 — Create your account

Open Rizqly and tap Create Account. Enter your email address and a secure password. You will be signed in automatically.

Tip: Use an email address you have long-term access to. Your account is the key to your committee data.

Step 2 — Sign in on a new device

If you reinstall Rizqly or get a new phone, open the app and sign in with the same email and password. Your committees, members, and records will be retrieved from the cloud automatically.

Account & Login

Rizqly uses Supabase for authentication. Your password is never stored in plain text. Your data is stored in a secure cloud database and is associated only with your account.

If you forget your password

On the login screen, tap Forgot Password. Enter your email address. You will receive a password reset link.

Signing out

To sign out, go to the Dashboard screen and use the Admin Controls panel, then tap Sign Out.

Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after signing in. It shows:

  • A summary of all your active committees and members
  • Total pool value across all committees
  • Overall contribution and payout overview
  • A rotating Quranic verse for daily inspiration
  • Quick access to Backup & Restore
  • Theme toggle (Light / Dark / System)

Admin Controls

The Admin Controls panel (accessible from the Dashboard) lets you sign out and access backup/restore functions.

Members

The Members screen shows everyone in your directory — across all committees.

Adding a member

  1. Go to Members from the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the Add Member button.
  3. Enter the member's full name and phone number (optional but recommended for WhatsApp).
  4. Tap Save.

Editing a member

Tap the member's name to open their details. Tap the edit icon to update their name or phone number.

Deleting a member

A member can be deleted from the edit screen. Note: deleting a member does not delete their historical contribution and payout records in existing committees.

Tip: Add all your members first before creating committees. You can then quickly assign them when setting up a new committee.

Committees

Creating a committee

  1. Go to Committees from the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap Create Committee.
  3. Enter the committee name.
  4. Set the contribution amount per member per cycle.
  5. Choose the cycle frequency (weekly, monthly, or custom).
  6. Configure payout order and other committee rules.
  7. Tap Create.

Committee Detail screen

Tap any committee to open its full detail screen. Here you can see:

  • All members and their contribution status
  • Current cycle information
  • Payout history
  • Full transaction ledger
  • PDF statement generation
Note: The committee configuration (amount, frequency) should be set correctly before the first cycle begins. Review carefully before proceeding.

Contributions

A contribution is a payment from a member for a specific cycle. Rizqly lets you record, view, and track these individually.

Recording a contribution

  1. Open the committee.
  2. Find the member in the current cycle's contribution list.
  3. Tap the member's row and record their payment.
  4. Enter the amount and date, then confirm.

Contribution status

Each contribution will show as Paid or Due. Overdue contributions are clearly flagged.

Tip: Record contributions on the day they are received to keep your ledger accurate.

Payouts

A payout is when a member receives the full collected pot for their assigned cycle.

Recording a payout

  1. Open the committee.
  2. Navigate to the Payouts section.
  3. Tap Record Payout.
  4. Select the receiving member, the cycle number, the payout amount, and the date.
  5. Confirm to save the record.

The payout record is permanent and appears in the committee's transaction ledger and PDF statement.

Financial Records

The Transactions screen shows a full chronological ledger of all contributions and payouts across all your committees. You can filter by type and date.

All records are stored securely in the cloud and are tied to your account. They are accessible on any device you sign in to.

Statements & PDFs

Rizqly can generate a professional PDF statement for any committee. The statement includes:

  • Committee name and configuration
  • All member contributions by cycle
  • All payouts recorded
  • Total collected and disbursed amounts

Generating a statement

  1. Open the committee.
  2. Tap Generate Statement (or the PDF icon).
  3. The PDF is created instantly.
  4. Tap Share to send it via WhatsApp, email, or save to your device.

Backup & Restore

Rizqly allows you to export a complete backup of your data as a JSON file and restore it later. This is useful when switching devices or as a safety copy.

Creating a backup

  1. Go to the Dashboard.
  2. Open Admin Controls.
  3. Tap Export Backup.
  4. Save the file to your device or share it to cloud storage (Google Drive, etc).

Restoring from backup

  1. Go to the Dashboard.
  2. Open Admin Controls.
  3. Tap Import Backup.
  4. Select your backup file.
  5. Confirm the restore. Existing data will be replaced.
Important: Restoring a backup replaces your current data. Make sure you have selected the correct backup file before confirming.
Cloud note: The backup is a local file export. Storing it in cloud storage (Google Drive, iCloud, etc.) is your responsibility. Rizqly's automated cloud backup is a planned future feature.

WhatsApp Notifications

Rizqly can send automated WhatsApp messages when contributions or payouts are recorded. This feature is powered by the Evolution API and is optional.

Current status

WhatsApp integration is available in the app but requires server-side configuration by the administrator. Individual users cannot configure WhatsApp connections from within the app in the current version.

If WhatsApp is not configured, Rizqly safely skips the notification step — no error will occur and your records will still be saved correctly.

Tip: All core functionality — recording contributions, payouts, generating statements, and backup — works completely without WhatsApp.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions

Having trouble? Try these solutions before reaching out for support.

Install APK won't install

Possible causes and solutions:

  • Enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser in Android Settings → Apps
  • Check the download completed fully — retry if the file seems incomplete
  • If you have an older version installed, try updating over it (don't uninstall first)
  • Ensure your Android version is 8.0 or higher

Login Can't sign in

Try these steps:

  • Double-check your email address and password for typos
  • Use the Forgot Password option to reset your password
  • Ensure your phone has an active internet connection
  • Wait a moment and try again — a temporary server issue may resolve itself

Data Data not appearing

If committees or members seem missing:

  • Confirm you are signed in with the correct account email
  • Pull down to refresh on the Committees or Members screen
  • Check your internet connection
  • Sign out and sign back in to force a data refresh

WhatsApp WhatsApp not sending

WhatsApp notifications are optional and require server configuration:

  • If the feature is not configured for your installation, notifications will silently skip — this is normal
  • All contribution and payout records are still saved correctly without WhatsApp
  • Contact your Rizqly administrator if WhatsApp was previously working and has stopped

Backup Backup restore failed

If restoring a backup fails:

  • Ensure the backup file is a .json file exported from Rizqly
  • Do not edit the backup file manually
  • Check that the file is not corrupted — re-export from your previous device if possible
  • Make sure you have enough storage space on your device

Update How to update the app

Rizqly is not on Google Play, so updates are manual:

  • Visit this page and check the Download section for the latest version
  • Download the new APK and install it over your current app
  • Your data is preserved — no need to uninstall first
  • Your account and all records remain after updating
