What is Rizqly?

Community Savings Made Simple

A ROSCA — also known as Kameti, Chit Fund, or Committee — is a group savings arrangement where every member contributes a fixed amount each cycle, and designated members receive payouts. Rizqly makes this completely paperless and transparent.

🏘️

Who is it for?

Community organisers, families, friends, and colleagues who run rotating savings groups and want a reliable digital record instead of paper ledgers or manual spreadsheets.

🎯

What problem does it solve?

Manual ROSCA tracking leads to disputes, forgotten payments, and missing records. Rizqly gives every committee a clear, timestamped ledger that all parties can trust.

📲

What does it run on?

Rizqly runs entirely on Android. It connects to your secure account in the cloud so your data is safe even if you switch phones. No desktop required.

Features

Everything You Need to Run a Committee

Rizqly covers every step of the ROSCA lifecycle — from onboarding members to generating the final financial statement.

🏛️

Committee Management

Create and manage multiple committees. Set contribution amounts, choose Monthly or Weekly schedules, and configure single or multiple payout slots per cycle.

👥

Member Registry

Build a permanent member directory with names and phone numbers. Reuse members across multiple committees without re-entering their details.

💳

Contribution Tracking

Record each member's payment for every cycle. Track weekly and monthly instalments with clear payment statuses.

💰

Payout Scheduling

Record which member receives the pot for each cycle slot. Track payout dates, amounts, and cycle numbers with a permanent timestamped record.

📊

Treasury Dashboard

Get a real-time overview of your Bait-ul-Maal treasury — total pool value, disbursed pots, active cycles, and pending contributions — on one screen.

📄

PDF Financial Statements

Generate a branded PDF statement for any committee with a single tap. Share it directly via WhatsApp, email, or any other app on your phone.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export a complete JSON backup of your data to your device at any time. Restore it instantly if you change phones or need to recover information.

💬

WhatsApp Receipts

Optionally send automated WhatsApp receipts to members when their contribution or payout is recorded — powered by Evolution API.

Installation Guide

How to Install Rizqly on Android

Installing an APK is simple. Follow these steps to get Rizqly running on your device.

1

Download the APK

Tap the "Download Rizqly APK" button in the Download section above. Your browser will save the file to your Downloads folder.

2

Open the downloaded file

Go to your Downloads app or file manager, find the APK file, and tap it to open.

3

Allow installation from this source

Android may display a prompt saying the app is from an unknown source. Tap Settings, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager, then return and try again.

4

Tap Install

Android will show you the app permissions. Tap Install to confirm. The installation takes a few seconds.

5

Open Rizqly

Tap Open on the installation screen, or find the Rizqly icon in your app drawer. Sign in to your account and you're ready to go.

⚠️ About the "Unknown Source" prompt:
Android shows a prompt when you install an app from outside Google Play. This is a standard Android security notification — it does not indicate an issue. Rizqly is distributed directly as an APK. You can safely allow the installation.
💡 Android version requirement:
Rizqly targets Android 8.0 (API 26) and above. Most Android devices from 2018 or later are compatible.
🔄 Updating Rizqly:
When a new version is released, download the latest APK from this page and install it over the existing app. Your data will be preserved.

Getting Started

Step 1 — Create your account

Open Rizqly and tap Create Account. Enter your email address and a secure password. You will be signed in automatically.

Tip: Use an email address you have long-term access to. Your account is the key to your committee data.

Step 2 — Sign in on a new device

If you reinstall Rizqly or get a new phone, open the app and sign in with the same email and password. Your committees, members, and records will be retrieved from the cloud automatically.

Account & Login

Rizqly uses Supabase for authentication. Your password is never stored in plain text. Your data is stored in a secure cloud database and is associated only with your account.

If you forget your password

On the login screen, tap Forgot Password. Enter your email address to receive a password reset link.

Signing out

To sign out, go to the Dashboard screen and use the Admin Controls panel, then tap Sign Out.

Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after signing in. It shows:

  • A summary of all your active committees and members
  • Total pool value across all committees
  • Overall contribution and payout overview
  • A rotating Quranic verse for daily inspiration
  • Quick access to Backup & Restore
  • Theme toggle (Light / Dark / System)

Members

The Members screen shows everyone in your directory — across all committees.

Adding a member

  1. Go to Members from the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the Add Member button.
  3. Enter the member's full name and phone number (optional but recommended for WhatsApp).
  4. Tap Save.

Editing a member

Tap the member's name to open their details. Tap the edit icon to update their name or phone number.

Committees

Creating a committee

  1. Go to Committees from the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap Create Committee.
  3. Enter the committee name.
  4. Set the contribution amount per member per cycle.
  5. Choose Monthly or Weekly frequency.
  6. Configure payout slots per cycle (1 or multiple).
  7. Tap Create.

Contributions

A contribution is a payment from a member for a specific cycle. Rizqly lets you record, view, and track these individually.

Recording a contribution

  1. Open the committee.
  2. Find the member in the current cycle's contribution list.
  3. Tap the member's row and record their payment.
  4. Enter the amount and date, then confirm.

Payouts

A payout is when a member receives the pot for their assigned cycle slot.

Recording a payout

  1. Open the committee.
  2. Navigate to the Payouts section.
  3. Tap Record Payout.
  4. Select the receiving member, cycle number, payout amount, and date.
  5. Confirm to save the record.

Financial Records

The Transactions screen shows a full chronological ledger of all contributions and payouts across all your committees.

Statements & PDFs

Rizqly can generate a branded PDF statement for any committee in one tap. Share it via WhatsApp or email.

Backup & Restore

Export a complete JSON backup of your data to your device at any time. Restore it instantly if you change phones.
